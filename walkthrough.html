Leisure Suit Jeff
The Complete Solution!

Get paid! Fix the Fiero! Remove Ohio! Our crack strategy team donned polyester, braved the break room and lived to tell the tale. Here is every single point in Act I: "In Search of the Lost Paycheck."

BY "DISCO" DAVE KOWALSKI & THE ELECTRO-GAMER STRATEGY SQUAD

Hold on to your bell-bottoms, gamers! Polyester Software's new parser adventure has been jamming our office phone lines since the day it hit store shelves. The number one question? "How do I get Merv's stapler?!" (Number two: "What does PC LOAD LETTER mean?" We're still not sure, either.)

For the uninitiated: you play Jeff Lovett, 44 years young and the proud owner of one powder-blue leisure suit. Tomorrow night is the Westside High reunion. Jeff's Pontiac Fiero is deader than disco, his suit has a chili stain shaped like the great state of Ohio, and his wallet holds a grand total of $12.75. Your mission: get your paycheck, fix the car, clean the suit and get a good night's sleep.

Like the Sierra classics it pays homage to, Leisure Suit Jeff uses a type-in parser. Type LOOK, TAKE, TALK TO and friends in plain English. Unlike those classics, you can also type GO TO COSTCO from anywhere and hop on the #42 bus. It costs $2.00 and half an hour of game time. Once the Fiero runs, DRIVE TO anywhere for free in 15 minutes.

The clock starts at 2:30 PM Friday, and every command burns a game minute. Don't panic: our route below wraps up before 6 PM, with hours to spare before Costco locks its doors at 8:30. Ready? Fire up your PC, warm up that AdLib card, and follow along. Remember: SAVE EARLY, SAVE OFTEN!

PRO TIP!

The parser understands more than you think. X MEMO examines, I shows inventory, and PICK UP THE TPS REPORT works as well as TAKE REPORT. You can even click the names under the picture to add them to your command!

Part 1: Synergex SolutionsFRIDAY, 2:30 PM · 43 POINTS

Your Cubicle

  1. READ MEMO +1 All TPS reports need the NEW cover sheet. No cover sheet, no paycheck. Mmm-kay?
  2. TAKE REPORT +2
  3. USE COMPUTER +2 You print the cover sheet... and get the dreaded PC LOAD LETTER.
  4. PLAY SOLITAIRE +1 Seriously! It's the most-missed point in the game. See "The Secret Point" below.

The Break Room

  1. GO TO BREAK ROOM
  2. KICK PRINTER +2 It won't fix anything, but boy does it feel good.
  3. OPEN FRIDGE Look, but do not touch. Trust us. (See Death Watch.)
  4. TAKE PAPER The ream is propping up the wobbly table. CRASH!
  5. PUT PAPER IN PRINTER +3
  6. TAKE COVER SHEET +2
  7. TAKE CAKE +2 Sorry, Deb.
  8. TAKE FLAIR, then WEAR FLAIR +2 Minimum flair achieved!

Merv's Cubicle & The Boss

  1. GO CUBICLES
  2. GIVE CAKE TO MERV +5 A corner piece! Merv lends you his precious orange stapler. (Try TAKE STAPLER first if you want to hear a dial-up modem in pain.)
  3. STAPLE REPORT +5 KA-CHUNK!
  4. GIVE STAPLER TO MERV +3 Be a pal. He'll smile for the first time in eleven years.
  5. GO TO BRUMLEY'S OFFICE and TALK TO BRUMLEY
  6. GIVE REPORT TO BRUMLEY +10 +$250.00 Cha-ching! Payroll is "doing a cash thing" this week.

The Lobby

  1. GO LOBBY
  2. TALK TO PEGGY +1 You'll be placed on hold. In person.
  3. TALK TO TODDS +2 Survive the consultants!

Death Watch!

It wouldn't be a Sierra-style adventure without some spectacular ways to buy the farm. Here's how NOT to play:

  • KAREN KNOWS: EAT YOGURT in the break room. She counted them.
  • DEATH BY DECAF: DRINK COFFEE. That pot has been brewing since 1997.
  • DEATH BY BRUT FORCE: DRINK COLOGNE. It goes on your neck, Jeff.
  • DEATH BY CONDIMENT: EAT MAYO at Costco. All five gallons.
  • EAU DE DEATH: DRINK the 1-gallon jug of cologne in the aisles.
  • NO BATTERY FOR YOU: Show up at Costco after 8:30 PM without a battery. Hello, bus to the reunion.

Bought the farm? Type UNDO to rewind one move. If only real life had this.

BUDGET BREAKDOWN

Paycheck ($250) plus couch quarters ($0.75) covers it all: membership $65, battery $79.99, hot dog $1.50 and three bus rides at $2 each. You'll finish Act I with $111.01 in your pocket. Lose the coupon and you're still fine, but it's two easy points!

Part 4: Victory LapBACK HOME · 30 POINTS

  1. TAKE BUS HOME +2 -$2.00 Jeff returns the cart to the corral like a decent human being. Civilization thanks you.
  2. PUT BATTERY IN CAR +10 VRRRROOM! The Fiero lives! One headlight pops up proudly.
  3. DRIVE TO WORK +3 Take her for a spin, then DRIVE HOME. It's free!
  4. GO APARTMENT
  5. SLEEP IN WATERBED +15 The waterbed sloshes in triumph. END OF ACT I!

The Secret Point

Finished with 129 points and pulling your hair out? (Careful, Jeff doesn't have much to spare.) The missing point is "productivity." Back at your cubicle, type PLAY SOLITAIRE. You win, the cards bounce, and it's the most fulfilling ten minutes of Jeff's work week. 130 of 130!

Got an AdLib Music Synthesizer Card? Crank it! The swingin' title theme "Polyester Nights" is pure lounge-lizard bliss. Every location has its own groove, from the bossa-nova hold music at Synergex to the "Bulk Buy Boogie" at Costco. Office-mates complaining? Type MUSIC OFF, SFX OFF, or VOLUME 3.

WHISPERS

Our spies at Polyester Software say Act II is in the works. Jeff will need a date for the reunion, plus some dignity and, apparently, ice. Our sources hint at a gym called Iron Paradise, a place named Lefty's Lounge and a trip to the Disco Inferno. We also noticed some very strange stuff in Act I, like "8K" TVs and a teenager calling Jeff's suit "lowkey fire." Time travel? A typo? We'll keep you posted, gamers!
